## Finding 1 — blocking, confirmed — spec wrongly scoped CI hard-enforcement as "not applicable"

Round 1's `mkr-security-reviewer` found that `specs/ReviewRecordChainFallback_Spec.md`'s original
§3/§4 claimed CI hard-enforcement of the review-record check was "not applicable to this fix;
this stays the WARN-only local hook" — false. `.github/workflows/mkr-gate.yml`'s "Require a G4
review record for this commit" step already hard-blocks (`exit 1`) on `push`/`pull_request` to
`main` using this exact `find_review_record`, with a real past CI failure already on record
(`.mkr/reviews/c4dfe34.md`). This meant the change's actual, most consequential blast radius (a
hard merge gate, not an advisory WARN) was assessed under a false premise through three rounds of
spec review and a design gate.

Independently re-verified before accepting (not taken on the reviewer's word): read
`.github/workflows/mkr-gate.yml` directly, confirmed the hard-blocking step and its
`find_review_record` call, and confirmed its checkout step already uses `fetch-depth: 0` (full
history) — meaning the bounded backward walk added by this fix resolves correctly there with no
shallow-clone gap, and no code-logic change was actually needed in `mkr-gate.yml` itself.

**Fix** (commit `e4d3cf1`): corrected `specs/ReviewRecordChainFallback_Spec.md` §3 (moved
`mkr-gate.yml`'s stale comment into scope as documentation-only) and §4 (states the real CI blast
radius plainly, cites `c4dfe34.md`, confirms `fetch-depth: 0`); corrected
`docs/adr/0008`'s Consequences to match; added `TC-RRF-16` so the comment can't regress silently
again. Re-verified independently by round 2's `mkr-security-reviewer`: re-confirmed `fetch-depth:
0` by direct read, re-audited `mkr-gate.yml`'s full invocation (TARGET_SHA/EXPECTED_PRIOR_TIP
derivation) for any other gap, found none, and confirmed `TC-RRF-16`'s assertion is a real
regression guard, not a tautology.
